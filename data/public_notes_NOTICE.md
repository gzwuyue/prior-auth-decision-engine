# Public notes notice

`public_notes.jsonl` contains three medical transcription samples from **MTSamples**
(https://www.mtsamples.com), a collection of transcribed sample reports that the site states may be
shared for educational purposes with credit to mtsamples.com.

| Request ID | MTSamples sample | Specialty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MTS-2085 | Osteoarthritis - Progress Note | Orthopedic |
| MTS-2177 | Leg Pain - Progress Note | Orthopedic |
| MTS-1290 | Weight Loss on Phentermine | SOAP / Chart / Progress Notes |

The text was taken from the public mtsamples.csv dataset and lightly reformatted (line breaks only).
Member IDs and the CPT/HCPCS codes on the generated fax pages are synthetic. These notes are
**unlabeled**: they demonstrate the pipeline on real-world clinical language and are not part of the
scored evaluation. Source: www.mtsamples.com.
